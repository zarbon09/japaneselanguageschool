Student visa, cost, and when to apply

A language-school student visa usually needs a Certificate of Eligibility, a recognized school, and enough funds. Intakes cluster around January, April, July, and October — applications often start six months earlier.

Read types of language schools and how to choose one, then the Tokyo and Osaka living-cost guides.

Questions people ask before they trust a matcher

Are you a study-abroad agency?

No. We help you shortlist and compare. You apply through the school (or an agent you choose). We do not hide that distinction.

How complete is the school list?

This catalog is Tokyo and Osaka only for now — 109 schools, not every language school in Japan. Other cities come later.

Do I need a student visa?

Full-time programs over a few months usually do. Working-holiday and other statuses can use short or part-time courses. The quiz treats visa as a hard filter, not a suggestion.

Can I work while I study?

Student-visa holders typically need permission to work part-time, with hour limits. Schools differ in how much they help. We flag housing and schedule intensity because those affect whether work is realistic.
